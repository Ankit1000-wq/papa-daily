Try it on something new

No machine for these. Use the race results and the ladder to think them through, then tell Papa.

  1. A new metal, ‘Mystery-ium’, fizzes in cold water, but more slowly than calcium. Where on the ladder would you guess it sits, and what would you expect to see in steam?
  2. Papa has a piece of metal that does nothing in cold water and nothing in hot water, but fizzes a little in steam. Name two metals it could be, and say how you decided.
  3. A water pipe at home is made of copper. Using what you saw today, why is copper a good choice, and why would a pipe made of sodium be a terrible idea?
  4. After a metal reacted, the water turned red litmus blue. A different metal reacted in steam and the litmus didn’t change. What do you think was different about what each one made?

Your answers stay on this device only. Show them to Papa when you’re done!
