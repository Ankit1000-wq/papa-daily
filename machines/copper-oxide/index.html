Hydrogen takes oxygen away

Hydrogen takes oxygen away from a metal oxide. Losing oxygen is reduction, and gaining it is oxidation.

Try it on something new

No machine for these. Use what you saw and tap on the see-saw to think them through, then tell Papa.

  1. A friend says, ‘The water drops came from damp gas, not from the experiment.’ What did Papa add to the set-up to settle this, and how would you explain it to her?
  2. Steel wool (iron) gets heavier when it rusts, but our black powder got lighter. Use the idea of oxygen moving to explain both.
  3. Zinc oxide stayed white and nothing happened. Look at the reactivity ladder. What pattern do you notice about which oxides hydrogen can reduce?
  4. A magnesium ribbon burns in air and leaves a white powder. Was the magnesium oxidised or reduced? How do you know?

Your answers stay on this device only. Show them to Papa when you’re done!
