Try it on something new

No machine for these. Use your clues to think them through, then tell Papa.

  1. Long ago, huge airships were filled with hydrogen, and one called the Hindenburg burst into flames in 1937. Why do you think people chose hydrogen to fill them, and what made it risky?
  2. You have three jars. One holds hydrogen, one oxygen and one carbon dioxide, but the labels fell off. Plan a test with a burning splint. What would you expect to see in each jar?
  3. You must carry an open jar of hydrogen across the room to Papa. How will you hold it, and why?
  4. Your friend says, ‘Hydrogen burns, so it must help things burn too.’ Which of today’s tests would you show her, and what would she see?

Your answers stay on this device only. Show them to Papa when you’re done!
